7 Communication Habits That Keep Marriages Resilient and Strong

By Dr. Sarah Jenkins, LMFT
Published on September 30, 2026 • 6 min read
Main Article Banner Image Place holder

Communication is often cited as the foundation of a successful relationship, but what does healthy day-to-day communication actually look like? In long-term marriages, deep connections aren't built on dramatic declarations, but rather on microscopic, regular habits that reduce friction and cultivate emotional safety.

Couples who navigate seasonal marital stress effectively don’t avoid disagreements. Instead, they share a mutual language rooted in respect. Below are the seven key habits marital therapists recommend establishing with your partner.

1. Practicing the "Soft Start-up"

How an argument begins typically dictates exactly how it will end. Research shows that bringing up complaints gently—without immediate blame—prevents your spouse's defensive nervous system response from overriding their logical thinking processes.

2. Intentional Micro-Validation

Validation doesn't mean agreeing entirely with everything your partner states. It simply means communicating that you understand their perspective. Phrases like, "I can see why that made you feel overwhelmed," function as conversational pressure valves during high-stress interactions.
