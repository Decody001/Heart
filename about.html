Our Story

Empowering couples to build resilient, lifelong connections.

Founder Photo

Welcome to Heart & Harmony. Founded in 2026, our platform was created to bridge the gap between academic relationship research and real-world daily marital struggles. We believe that maintaining a healthy connection shouldn't rely on guesswork, but on proven, intentional communication habits.

Whether you are navigating the early challenges of your first year of marriage, adjusting to parenting milestones, or working to restore trust after an emotional disconnection, our expert-backed resources offer actionable pathways toward healing and growth.

Our Core Mission

"To lower global divorce rates by making professional, evidence-based relationship therapy insights accessible, fast-loading, and completely practical for every household."

Thank you for letting us be a small part of your relationship journey. Remember, a resilient bond isn't built on a single grand gesture—it is cultivated through micro-moments of everyday care, listening, and validation.
